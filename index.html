About this plan

For individuals taking their first steps towards psychological support or seeking guidance with current emotional challenges. Explore present concerns, identify emotional triggers and begin developing healthier responses.

What's included

  • Initial exploration of presenting concerns and collaborative goal setting
  • Understanding emotional triggers, thoughts and responses
  • Introduction to practical coping and emotional regulation strategies
  • Guided reflection exercises and relevant therapeutic worksheets
  • Personalised therapeutic direction based on initial sessions
  • Progress check-in within the package

May suit people experiencing

Everyday stress, emotional overwhelm, anxiety, adjustment difficulties, relationship concerns or those seeking an introduction to professional therapy.

About this plan

A structured therapeutic experience for understanding the emotional and behavioural patterns influencing everyday life. Explore recurring thoughts and underlying beliefs while developing healthier responses.

What's included

  • Initial clinical screening and collaborative therapy goals
  • Identification of recurring emotional and behavioural patterns
  • CBT-informed interventions and cognitive restructuring, where appropriate
  • Exploration of underlying beliefs and relationship patterns
  • Personalised therapeutic worksheets and reflection exercises
  • Emotional regulation and coping strategies
  • Progress reviews and adjustment of goals as needed

May suit people experiencing

Anxiety, relationship difficulties, low self-esteem, emotional regulation challenges, career-related stress, negative thought patterns or difficulties with personal boundaries.

SUSTAINED SUPPORT

Transform

Deeper understanding. Sustainable growth.
₹12,000 / package
22 sessionsIndividual therapy9 weeksValidity45–50 minPer sessionDeeper workOngoing support

About this plan

Balenza's most comprehensive therapeutic package, for individuals seeking consistent and deeper psychological support. Explore longstanding emotional difficulties and work towards meaningful psychological and behavioural change at an individual pace.

What's included

  • Detailed exploration of presenting concerns and relevant personal history
  • Individualised therapeutic roadmap and ongoing goal review
  • Exploration of core beliefs and longstanding emotional patterns
  • CBT and psychodynamic-informed interventions, as clinically appropriate
  • Trauma-informed work where indicated and within the scope of care
  • Attachment, relationship and boundary exploration where relevant
  • Emotional regulation, behavioural change strategies and personalised exercises
  • Progress reviews and maintenance planning, where appropriate

May suit people seeking support with

Longstanding emotional difficulties, childhood experiences, relationship and attachment concerns, trauma-related difficulties, persistent anxiety, self-esteem concerns or recurring behavioural patterns.
